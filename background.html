Mathematical and academic background

My preparation combines electrical engineering, machine learning, and teaching in mathematics and engineering. Linear algebra, probabilistic modeling, and the analysis of signals and systems connect this training to my interests in learning, inference, and decision-making.

Mathematical preparation

I have designed homework for Linear Algebra and Engineering Mathematics, and homework and exams for Signals & Systems. These responsibilities involve formulating mathematical problems and helping students work through the concepts behind their solutions.

My projects have included Bayesian-network inference by variable elimination and sampling, Gaussian mixture models fitted by expectation-maximization, and PCA for reconstruction. Search and constraint-solving work has covered graph algorithms, minimax, constraint propagation, and CP-SAT. In reinforcement learning, I combined theoretical study with the evaluation of value-based and policy-gradient methods. Together, these experiences give me a foundation for moving between probabilistic, algebraic, and algorithmic formulations of learning problems.

My mathematical notes develop worked analyses of policy evaluation from historical data, rare-event simulation, and adaptive sensor calibration. They include full derivations, exact examples, and limits imposed by the available information, alongside supporting probability notes and a handwritten calculation in analysis.
