Selected projects

These projects extend my research experience into implementation across reasoning, decision-making, and generative modeling. They involve building the surrounding training and evaluation pipelines as well as the models themselves.

GitHub profile

LLM reasoning and neuro-symbolic AI

I built systems that generate programs and evaluate them through execution, using sequence models and Transformers. Related work included symbolic regression, tool-augmented inference, and search over candidate reasoning paths, including Tree of Thoughts, A*, and Monte Carlo tree search.

I also implemented retrieval-augmented and graph-based pipelines with entity graphs, clustering, and structured evaluation. These projects connect language-model outputs to executable or structured representations, giving me experience with several ways to organize and assess multi-step reasoning.

PyTorch · Transformers · TRL · vLLM · NetworkX · SymPy

Reinforcement learning algorithms and systems

I implemented and evaluated methods from temporal-difference learning through deep actor-critic algorithms, including DQN variants, PPO, SAC, and DDPG. I extended this work to imitation learning, offline learning, multi-agent systems, and hierarchical control.

The projects combine theoretical study with implementation and empirical evaluation across several reinforcement-learning settings. They complement my teaching experience in Deep Reinforcement Learning and my interest in how learning algorithms support sequential decisions.

Python · PyTorch · Gymnasium · NumPy

Generative models and adaptation

I built learning systems for vision, language, and temporal prediction, alongside fine-tuning workflows using LoRA and prefix tuning. My generative-modeling work included VAEs, DDPMs, and latent diffusion.

I also investigated learned representations and visualized attention in vision-language models. This work combines model construction and adaptation with examination of the representations and attention patterns learned by the models.

PyTorch · TensorFlow · Transformers · Diffusers

Persian automatic speech recognition

I developed a Persian speech recognition system using a Conformer-Transducer architecture. The work covered audio normalization and preprocessing, training on segmented speech, and evaluation across speakers and acoustic conditions.

The project brought data preparation, model training, and recognition evaluation into one pipeline, extending my experience with language beyond text generation to speech.

Python · PyTorch · torchaudio
