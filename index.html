Mohammad Pouya Toroghi

M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering · Sharif University of Technology

I work on the security and reliability of machine learning systems, with a current focus on large language models. I am a master's student at Sharif University of Technology, advised by Mohammad Hossein Rohban in the RIML Lab.

My research spans backdoor detection, semantic calibration, and machine unlearning. Across these projects, I develop methods and experimental pipelines to examine model behavior under explicit assumptions. My contributions range from problem formulation and method development to baseline comparisons and evaluation.

I bring an electrical engineering background and experience building systems for program synthesis, tool-augmented reasoning, reinforcement learning, and speech recognition. These projects have given me experience with both algorithm design and the practical work of training and evaluation.

PhD interests

My long-term interest is artificial general intelligence: how a system can search over possible solutions, assess intermediate reasoning, and decide where to spend computation. I want to connect uncertainty estimation with reinforcement learning and neuro-symbolic methods, starting with problems where the quality of a solution can be checked.

I am seeking PhD opportunities beginning in Fall 2027.
